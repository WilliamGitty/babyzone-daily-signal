    IPPR proposes £3.8bn annual shift from Whitehall to English mayors via income tax share Fiscal devolution and regional commissioning landscape★★

    IPPR and IPPR North have published a report proposing that England's mayors receive a 5% share of income tax revenues, shifting an estimated £3.8 billion per year out of Whitehall and roughly doubling devolved funding. The report, endorsed by Lord Jim O'Neill, calls for built-in accountability safeguards including regional audit offices and a tariff and top-up fairness mechanism, and broadly supports plans for fiscal devolution to all mayors by 2028.

    Why it matters to Babyzone: If fiscal devolution to combined authorities accelerates and relevant DfE or MHCLG budgets move into mayoral control, the landscape for how charities like Babyzone access public early years funding in areas with elected mayors could shift meaningfully — worth monitoring as a background strategic risk and opportunity.
