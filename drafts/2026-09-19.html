    Record £370m in dormant assets earmarked for good causes following policy change Dormant assets / charitable funding landscape★★★★ high

    A record £370 million from the dormant assets scheme has been set aside for distribution to good causes in the UK after a policy change expanded the scheme's scope. In England, dormant assets funds flow through the National Lottery Community Fund (NLCF) as the distributing body.

    Why it matters to Babyzone: NLCF is a named watchlist funder for Babyzone; a significant uplift of this scale to the dormant assets pot could open new or enlarged NLCF grant programmes relevant to community early-years organisations, making it worth tracking closely ahead of any forthcoming programme announcements.
